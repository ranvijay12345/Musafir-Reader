## Information stored on your device

Musafir Reader saves the following **only on your device** and never uploads it to us:

- Your reading progress for each book (which page you're on), saved separately
  for each language.
- Your preferences: reading language, light/dark theme, and text size.

Uninstalling the app removes this data. We do not operate accounts and do not
collect your name, email, or contacts.

## Advertising

Musafir Reader displays ads using **Google AdMob**. To show ads, the Google Mobile Ads
SDK may collect and process certain information, which can include:

- Device advertising identifiers,
- General device information and approximate, coarse location,
- Ad interaction data.

This information is handled by Google in accordance with Google's own privacy
policy. Please review:

- Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- How Google uses information from sites/apps that use its services:
  https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

You can limit ad personalization at any time through your device settings
(for example, **Settings ▸ Google ▸ Ads** on Android) by resetting or opting
out of your advertising ID.

## Children

Musafir Reader is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under
the age required by law in your region. We do not knowingly collect personal
information from children.

## Data sharing

We do not sell your personal information. The only third party that receives
data is Google, through the AdMob advertising service described above.

## Security

Data stored on your device is protected by your device's own security. Data
transmitted for ads is sent over encrypted connections.
